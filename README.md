Intercos S.p.A. — Financial Analysis & Peer Benchmarking

Project Overview

This project analyses the financial performance of Intercos S.p.A. through financial ratio analysis, trend analysis and peer benchmarking.

The project combines a 2015–2024 financial review of Intercos with a cross-sectional comparison against Salvatore Ferragamo, Acqua di Parma and KIKO Milano.

The objective was to evaluate profitability, liquidity, capital structure and financial stability, and translate the results into clear business insights.

Analysis Scope
The project focused on:
- Profitability and margin analysis
- Liquidity ratios
- Leverage and capital structure
- Balance-sheet structure
- Operating and financial performance
- Peer benchmarking
- Financial risk and business interpretation

Key Insights

Profitability

Intercos showed greater profitability volatility than the comparison companies, although performance was strong during its better-performing periods.

Liquidity

Liquidity was one of the main areas of concern. The project identified significant fluctuations in Intercos' liquidity ratios, while the selected competitors generally showed greater stability.
Capital Structure
Intercos demonstrated relatively strong equity backing and a more robust long-term capital structure compared with some peers.

Peer Comparison

The analysis showed that Intercos has a different financial profile from the selected competitors because it operates primarily as a cosmetics manufacturer, while the peer group includes luxury, fragrance and retail-focused businesses.

Overall Conclusion

The analysis suggests that Intercos has a strong long-term financial position and equity base, but faces challenges related to short-term liquidity and profitability volatility.
The project highlighted the importance of improving liquidity management while maintaining the company's stronger capital structure and operational performance.
